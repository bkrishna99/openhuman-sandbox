The pitch

Wayfinder doesn't replace TFI or Google Maps.

Wayfinder: discovers the options people don't know they have.

TFI / official sources: provide transport truth and live signals where permitted.

Google Maps: handles precise routing and navigation.

JEV: explains and ranks complex choices.

Network effect: every useful community hack makes the local mobility graph better for the next person.

Prototype note: TFI status, fares and journey times shown here are simulated demo signals. The Google Maps buttons use a standard Maps Directions URL for the A→B hand-off. Production would use permitted/official data integrations rather than scrape apps.

AI cost control

JEV is a selective reasoning layer
1 · Retrieve locally
Find 20 candidate options from the local mobility knowledge base.

2 · Filter with rules
Remove options that fail time, cost, eligibility or live-service constraints.

3 · Call JEV only for ambiguity
Give it the top 3–5 structured candidates and the user's preferences — not the entire internet.

4 · Cache the result
Store reusable rankings/decision patterns so the same question doesn't repeatedly require AI.
Integration model

Wayfinder doesn't replace TFI or Google Maps.

Wayfinder: local schemes + community knowledge + eligibility + ranking.
TFI/live feeds: current transport state, where available and licensed.
Google Maps: precise routing/navigation and live traffic where permitted.
JEV: explains and ranks complex choices.

The pitch: “We're building the intelligence layer that helps existing transport apps discover the options people don't know they have.”

Prototype note: live TFI status, fares and travel times shown here are simulated demo signals. The architecture is designed to replace them with licensed/official live data rather than scrape the TFI or Google Maps apps.

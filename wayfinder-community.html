💶 Fare hack
Use Leap where applicable
For Dublin-area DART/commuter rail and eligible city connections, compare Leap fares and caps rather than assuming cash fare.
Official rule: TFI says Leap can save up to 30% on DART/Commuter fares, and Zone 1 has daily/weekly caps of €6/€24 for adults on applicable services.
